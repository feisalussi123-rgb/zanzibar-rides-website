GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Do I need a driving licence?+

Yes. Bring a valid national or international driving licence and your passport or ID.

Can you deliver to my hotel?+

Yes. Delivery is free in Stone Town, Nungwi, Kendwa and Kiwengwa. Ask us about other areas.

Is a deposit required?+

A refundable deposit is required at handover. We explain all terms before you confirm.

Can I extend my rental?+

Usually, yes. Message us on WhatsApp before your return date and we will check availability.
