FAQ

Good to know before you book

Do I need a driving license? +

Yes — a valid national or international driving license is required for both cars and scooters.

Can you deliver the vehicle to my hotel? +

Yes, free delivery is included within Stone Town, Nungwi, Kendwa and Kiwengwa. Other areas may include a small fee.

What documents are required? +

A passport or ID and a valid driving license. A refundable deposit is also required.

Can I extend my rental? +

Yes, message us on WhatsApp before your return date and we'll extend it, subject to availability.

What happens if I return late? +

A grace period of one hour applies. After that, a late fee is charged per additional hour.
